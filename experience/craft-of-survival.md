## Overview

**Craft of Survival** is a single-player survival game with RPG elements set in a fantasy world, supported as a live-service title with regular content updates.

I worked on the project at 101XP in parallel with The Braves. My main area was adding new content to the game as part of its regular updates — getting designed content into the build correctly and on schedule.

## What it gave me

- Experience with the content side of a live mobile game, where updates ship on a steady cadence.
- A close look at how content moves from design tables into the game — the same problem I later automated on The Braves.
