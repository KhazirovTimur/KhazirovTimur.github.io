## Overview

**Craft of Survival** is a single-player survival game with RPG elements set in a fantasy world, supported as a live-service title with regular content updates.

It was my first project as a game designer at 101XP, right after moving over from QA. My main area was adding new content for its regular updates — getting designed content into the build correctly and on schedule.

## Shipping a major update with a team of two

**Situation.** After the decision to pause support for the project, most of the team left. A fairly large update was still unfinished, and it had to be released anyway. Two game designers remained — me and one colleague.

**What I did.** Much of the remaining work was outside a designer’s usual scope, so I took it on myself in Unity:

- fixed location bugs — collisions, NavMesh, enemy spawners and other level issues;
- worked through the studio’s internal tools that nobody had time to teach me, figuring them out on the fly;
- kept testing and fixing until the content was stable enough to ship.

**Result.** The update **was released successfully**. This is where my habit of learning the engine beyond design paid off: when there was nobody to hand a task to, I could still do it. After the release I moved on to The Braves.

## What it gave me

- Hands-on Unity level work under real release pressure: collisions, navigation and spawning.
- Confidence picking up unfamiliar internal tools quickly, without onboarding.
- Experience with the content side of a live mobile game, where updates ship on a steady cadence.
